Softly — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026

Softly is a companion app for Ravelry, made independently. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Ravelry.

The short version

Your data stays on your device or on Ravelry. Softly runs no databases, no analytics, no trackers, and no ads, and never sells or shares your information.

Signing in

You sign in with your existing Ravelry account using OAuth. Softly never sees or stores your Ravelry password. A small Softly server exchanges the sign-in code for access tokens and refreshes them when they expire; it holds no user accounts, stores no tokens, and keeps no record of your activity. Your tokens are stored only in your device's secure keychain.

Your Ravelry data

Your patterns, favorites, queue, yarn stash, needle inventory, and projects travel directly between your device and Ravelry's servers. Softly keeps a copy on your device so the app works offline. Actions you take in Softly (starting or finishing a project, adding yarn to your stash, adding a pattern to your library) are changes made to your own Ravelry account, governed by Ravelry's terms and privacy policy.

Photos

If you choose to add a photo to a finished project, that photo is uploaded to your Ravelry account. Softly accesses only the photo you pick and only when you pick it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Pattern files

Pattern PDFs you download or import are stored only on your device. You can remove any of them in the app.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes your tokens from the device. Deleting the app removes everything Softly stored locally. Your Ravelry account and its data are yours to manage at ravelry.com, and you can revoke Softly's access at any time from your Ravelry account settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: hello@softly.tools.
